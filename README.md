# Cheapy

**Your money, tracked simply — and never leaves your device.**

Cheapy is a free budget tracker that lives entirely in your browser. There's nothing to
sign up for, no account to create, and no company on the other end collecting your
financial history. Everything you enter — every peso in, every peso out — stays stored
locally on the phone, tablet, or computer you're using, for as long as you keep it there.

**Try it now:** [exho-g.github.io/cheapy-pub](https://exho-g.github.io/cheapy-pub/)

No install required to try it in a browser tab — and if you like it, your browser will
offer to add it to your home screen or desktop like a regular app, so it opens instantly
and works even without an internet connection.

---

## What you can do with it

**Track where your money lives.** Add your debit accounts, e-wallets, cash on hand, and
credit cards. Cheapy shows real bank and e-wallet logos so your accounts are easy to
recognize at a glance, and never asks for card numbers, expiry dates, or anything else
sensitive — it only needs to know the accounts exist, not how to access them.

**Log expenses, income, and transfers in seconds.** A clean, fast entry form, with
support for saving your frequent transactions (like a daily coffee or a monthly toll
top-up) as one-tap templates for next time.

**Never lose track of a bill or installment.** Recurring bills and fixed-term
installment plans are tracked automatically and show up right when they're due — with a
clear heads-up before anything becomes overdue.

**Stay on top of your credit cards.** Cheapy follows each card's own billing cycle,
groups your charges by statement period, tells you what's due and when, and lets you
mark a statement as paid once you've settled it.

**See the full picture on one Dashboard.** Monthly income vs. expense totals, spending
trends over time, a breakdown by category, a "safe to spend" figure for the rest of the
month, and a friendly heads-up for anything coming due — the moment you open the app, no
digging required.

**Get plain-language insights about your habits.** Cheapy quietly watches your pace of
spending, your budgets, and your goals, and surfaces useful, honest observations — like
whether you're on track to overspend this month — without ever sending your data
anywhere to do it. An optional, fully on-device AI chat can also answer questions about
your own numbers, if you choose to turn it on.

**Set savings goals and watch your progress.** Give a goal a name, a target amount, and
a date, and Cheapy tells you honestly whether your current saving pace will get you
there.

**Snap a photo of a receipt.** Instead of typing everything by hand, Cheapy can read the
amount, date, and merchant off a receipt photo and pre-fill the entry for you — you
still review and confirm every detail before it's saved.

**Print or export a clean account statement.** Generate a nicely formatted, printable
statement for any month (debit accounts) or billing cycle (credit cards) — complete with
totals, due dates, and payment status — or export the same data as a CSV file for your
own spreadsheets.

**Browse current bank and e-wallet promos.** A curated, regularly refreshed feed of
ongoing credit card and e-wallet deals, credited to its original source.

**Quick actions from your home screen.** On supported devices, press and hold (or
right-click) the Cheapy icon to jump straight into adding a transaction, or straight to
Insights, Promos, Goals, Bills, or Installments — no need to open the app first.

**Make it yours.** Light and dark themes, a choice of accent colors, a currency symbol
of your choice, and a full interface in nine languages: English, Filipino, Spanish,
French, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Thai, and Chinese.

**Lock it down.** An optional PIN keeps the app private on a shared device — nothing
fancy, just a simple gate so a passerby can't casually open your finances.

**Back up and restore on your own terms.** Since there's no cloud account, a single
export file is how you move your data to a new device or protect against loss — entirely
your choice, entirely in your hands.

## Why it's built this way

Most budget apps ask you to hand your financial life to a server somewhere. Cheapy
doesn't, on purpose:

- **No sign-up, no login, no account.** There's nothing to hack because there's no
  account to hack.
- **No cloud sync, no hidden server.** Your data is stored only on the device you're
  using it on. Nothing is uploaded automatically, ever.
- **Works without internet.** Once loaded, Cheapy keeps working offline — on a plane, in
  a spot with no signal, anywhere.
- **You decide what leaves the device.** A small number of optional features (searching
  for a bank logo, browsing promos, or turning on the AI chat) reach out to the internet
  — and only when you explicitly ask them to. Your transactions, balances, and personal
  notes are never part of those requests.

## Please read before you rely on it

- **This is not a bank, and it is not connected to any bank.** Every figure you see
  comes from what you've typed in yourself. Cheapy has no way to verify it against your
  real account balances, and nothing it produces — including the printable statements —
  is an official document from any financial institution.
- **This is not financial advice.** The insights, projections, and "safe to spend"
  figures are simple, automated estimates meant to be a helpful nudge, not professional
  guidance. Please use your own judgment for real financial decisions.
- **Backing up your data is your responsibility.** Because everything lives only on your
  device, clearing your browser data, resetting your device, or losing it can mean
  losing your records too. Export a backup regularly, and store it somewhere safe.
- **Bank, e-wallet, and card network logos** shown in the app belong to their respective
  owners and are used only so you can recognize your own accounts at a glance. Cheapy is
  not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any bank, e-wallet provider, or card
  network.
- **The Promos page** displays a snapshot of publicly available promotions credited to
  KasKasan Buddies, with a link back to their original page. Cheapy is not affiliated
  with them, and cannot guarantee a promo is still valid — always confirm with the bank
  or merchant directly before relying on it.
- **The optional AI chat**, only if you turn it on yourself, downloads a small language
  model to run directly on your device. It answers using only your own already-visible
  numbers, and nothing about your finances is sent anywhere to make it work — but like
  any automated feature, its answers can be wrong, so treat them as a starting point,
  not a final word.
- **Use at your own risk.** Cheapy is provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind.
